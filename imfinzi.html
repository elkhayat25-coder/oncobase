PD-L1 Immune Checkpoint Inhibitor

Imfinzi (durvalumab)

AstraZeneca

Imfinzi (durvalumab) is a human monoclonal antibody that blocks PD-L1 and restores anti-tumor immune responses by activating T cells.

Brand

Imfinzi

Generic

Durvalumab

Company

AstraZeneca

Route

IV Infusion

Approval

FDA Approved

Indications

  • Unresectable Stage III NSCLC after concurrent chemoradiotherapy
  • Extensive-stage Small Cell Lung Cancer (ES-SCLC)
  • Biliary Tract Cancer (with GemCis)
  • Hepatocellular Carcinoma (with Tremelimumab)

Mechanism of Action

Durvalumab is a human IgG1 monoclonal antibody that binds to PD-L1, preventing its interaction with PD-1 and CD80 receptors. This restores T-cell activation and enhances the immune system's ability to recognize and destroy cancer cells.

Dosing

Recommended dose: 1500 mg IV every 4 weeks.

Dosage & Administration

Recommended Dose 1500 mg IV every 4 weeks
Route Intravenous Infusion
Infusion Time Approximately 60 minutes
Premedication Not routinely required

Dose Modifications

  • Withhold treatment for Grade 3 immune-mediated adverse reactions.
  • Permanently discontinue for Grade 4 immune-related toxicities.
  • Resume treatment after recovery to Grade 1 or baseline when appropriate.

Monitoring

  • Liver Function Tests (LFTs)
  • Thyroid Function Tests (TFTs)
  • Pulmonary symptoms (Pneumonitis)
  • Immune-mediated adverse reactions

Drug Interactions

  • Systemic corticosteroids (before treatment initiation)
  • Other immunosuppressive agents
  • Live vaccines should be avoided during treatment
