EGFR / MET Bispecific Antibody

Rybrevant (amivantamab)

Johnson & Johnson

Rybrevant is a fully human bispecific antibody targeting EGFR and MET for the treatment of EGFR-mutated non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), particularly EGFR Exon 20 insertion mutations.

Brand

Rybrevant

Generic

Amivantamab

Company

Johnson & Johnson

Route

Intravenous

Approval

FDA Approved

Indications

  • Metastatic NSCLC with EGFR Exon 20 insertion mutations.
  • Advanced EGFR-mutated NSCLC after progression.
  • Combination therapy with Lazertinib in selected patients.

Mechanism of Action

Amivantamab is a bispecific antibody that simultaneously binds EGFR and MET receptors, blocking downstream signaling pathways, promoting receptor degradation, and inducing immune-mediated tumor cell killing.

Dose Modifications

  • Interrupt infusion for Grade ≥3 toxicity.
  • Resume at reduced infusion rate when appropriate.
  • Permanently discontinue for severe ILD.

Monitoring

  • Infusion reactions.
  • Pulmonary symptoms.
  • Skin toxicity.
  • Liver function tests.

Drug Interactions

  • No major CYP-mediated interactions.
  • Monitor with other pulmonary toxic agents.
  • Follow prescribing information for combination therapy.
