EGFR Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitor

Lazcluze (lazertinib)

Johnson & Johnson

Lazcluze is a third-generation EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitor indicated for EGFR-mutated metastatic non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), in combination with Rybrevant.

Brand

Lazcluze

Generic

Lazertinib

Company

Johnson & Johnson

Route

Oral

Approval

FDA Approved

Indications

  • Metastatic EGFR-mutated NSCLC
  • Exon 19 deletion
  • L858R mutation
  • Used in combination with Rybrevant

Mechanism of Action

Lazertinib is a third-generation irreversible EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitor that selectively inhibits EGFR activating mutations while demonstrating activity against the T790M resistance mutation.

Dose Modifications

  • Interrupt treatment for Grade ≥3 adverse reactions.
  • Resume after recovery according to prescribing information.
  • Discontinue permanently if severe toxicity recurs.

Monitoring

  • Skin toxicity
  • QT interval
  • Liver function tests
  • Interstitial lung disease / Pneumonitis

Drug Interactions

  • Strong CYP3A4 Inducers
  • QT-Prolonging medications
  • Avoid concomitant use when possible
