EGFR Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitor

Tagrisso (osimertinib)

AstraZeneca

Tagrisso is a third-generation EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitor indicated for the treatment of EGFR-mutated non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC).

Brand

Tagrisso

Generic

Osimertinib

Company

AstraZeneca

Route

Oral

Approval

FDA Approved

Indications

  • Metastatic EGFR-mutated NSCLC
  • Adjuvant EGFR-mutated NSCLC
  • Exon 19 deletion
  • L858R mutation

Mechanism of Action

Osimertinib is a third-generation, irreversible EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitor that selectively inhibits both EGFR sensitizing mutations (Exon 19 deletion and L858R) and the T790M resistance mutation while sparing wild-type EGFR.

Dosage & Administration

Recommended Dose 80 mg once daily
Route Oral
With Food With or without food
Missed Dose Skip if next dose is due within 12 hours.

Dose Modifications

  • Interrupt treatment for Grade ≥3 adverse reactions.
  • Resume at 80 mg or reduce to 40 mg if necessary.
  • Permanently discontinue for confirmed ILD.

Monitoring

  • ECG and QT interval
  • LVEF assessment
  • Signs of ILD / Pneumonitis
  • CBC and Liver Function Tests

Drug Interactions

  • Strong CYP3A4 Inducers
  • QT-Prolonging medications
  • Live vaccines (avoid)
