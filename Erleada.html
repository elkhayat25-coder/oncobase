Androgen Receptor Inhibitor

Erleada (apalutamide)

Janssen / Johnson & Johnson

Erleada (apalutamide) is an oral androgen receptor inhibitor used in the treatment of prostate cancer. It directly inhibits androgen receptor signaling by preventing androgen receptor activation, nuclear translocation, and transcriptional activity, thereby suppressing androgen-dependent prostate cancer growth.

Indications

  • Metastatic castration-sensitive prostate cancer (mCSPC).
  • Non-metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (nmCRPC).
  • Patients should receive a GnRH analog concurrently unless they have undergone bilateral orchiectomy.

Mechanism of Action

Apalutamide is a selective androgen receptor inhibitor. It binds directly to the androgen receptor and inhibits androgen receptor activation, nuclear translocation, DNA binding, and transcription of androgen-responsive genes. This suppresses androgen receptor signaling and inhibits prostate cancer cell proliferation and survival.

Dose Modifications

  • Withhold treatment for Grade 3 or 4 or intolerable adverse reactions.
  • Resume treatment at the same dose or a reduced dose after recovery.
  • Reduced doses may include 180 mg or 120 mg once daily.
  • Permanently discontinue treatment if a seizure occurs.
  • Monitor and manage significant cardiovascular events and fractures.

Monitoring

  • Blood pressure.
  • Signs and symptoms of cardiovascular events.
  • Risk of falls and fractures.
  • Signs and symptoms of seizures.
  • Skin reactions including severe cutaneous adverse reactions.
  • Signs and symptoms of interstitial lung disease/pneumonitis.
  • PSA levels and disease progression.
