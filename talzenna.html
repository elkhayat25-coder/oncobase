PARP Inhibitor

Talzenna (talazoparib)

Pfizer

Talzenna (talazoparib) is an oral PARP inhibitor that blocks PARP-mediated DNA repair and strongly promotes PARP trapping on DNA. It is used in combination with enzalutamide for selected patients with homologous recombination repair (HRR) gene-mutated metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer.

Indications

  • HRR gene-mutated metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC).
  • Used in combination with enzalutamide.
  • Requires identification of an appropriate HRR gene alteration according to the approved indication.

Mechanism of Action

Talazoparib inhibits PARP1 and PARP2 enzymatic activity and strongly traps PARP proteins on DNA. This interferes with DNA repair and replication. In tumors with homologous recombination repair deficiencies, particularly BRCA-related alterations, the accumulation of DNA damage results in synthetic lethality and cancer cell death.

Dosing

Recommended dose: Talazoparib 0.5 mg orally once daily in combination with enzalutamide.

Dose Modifications

  • Interrupt treatment for significant hematologic toxicity.
  • Resume at a reduced dose after recovery.
  • Dose adjustment may be required for renal impairment.
  • Monitor closely for anemia, neutropenia, and thrombocytopenia.
