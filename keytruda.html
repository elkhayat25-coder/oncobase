PD-1 Immune Checkpoint Inhibitor

Keytruda (pembrolizumab)

MSD

Keytruda is a programmed death receptor-1 (PD-1) blocking antibody used in multiple cancers including advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC).

Brand

Keytruda

Generic

Pembrolizumab

Company

MSD

Route

IV Infusion

Approval

FDA Approved

Indications

  • Metastatic NSCLC with PD-L1 expression
  • First-line monotherapy in high PD-L1 tumors
  • Combination therapy with platinum-based chemotherapy
  • Adjuvant and neoadjuvant settings in selected patients

Mechanism of Action

Pembrolizumab is a humanized monoclonal antibody that binds to the PD-1 receptor on T lymphocytes, preventing interaction with PD-L1 and PD-L2. This restores T-cell activation and enhances the immune system's ability to recognize and destroy cancer cells.

Dosing

Recommended dose: 200 mg IV every 3 weeks or 400 mg IV every 6 weeks.

Dose Modifications

  • Withhold for Grade 3 immune-related adverse reactions.
  • Permanently discontinue for Grade 4 toxicity.
  • Resume when toxicity improves to Grade 1 or less.

Monitoring

  • Liver function tests
  • Renal function
  • Thyroid function
  • Pulmonary symptoms
  • Immune-related adverse events

Drug Interactions

  • Systemic corticosteroids may reduce efficacy if used before treatment.
  • Other immunosuppressive agents should be used cautiously.
  • Avoid live vaccines during therapy.
