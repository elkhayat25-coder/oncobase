Lynparza (olaparib)

AstraZeneca

Lynparza (olaparib) is an oral PARP inhibitor that blocks PARP-mediated DNA repair. It is used in selected patients with homologous recombination repair (HRR) gene-mutated metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC), including BRCA-mutated disease.

Indications

  • HRR gene-mutated metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC) after progression following prior enzalutamide or abiraterone therapy.
  • BRCA-mutated metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer in combination with abiraterone and prednisone or prednisolone.
  • Patients should be selected based on an appropriate FDA-approved companion diagnostic.

Mechanism of Action

Olaparib inhibits poly (ADP-ribose) polymerase enzymes, including PARP1 and PARP2, which play important roles in DNA damage repair. PARP inhibition increases DNA damage and can lead to cancer cell death, particularly in tumors with homologous recombination repair deficiencies such as BRCA1 or BRCA2 mutations.

Dosing

Recommended dose: 300 mg orally twice daily, with or without food.

Dose Modifications

  • Interrupt treatment for significant adverse reactions.
  • Resume treatment at a reduced dose after recovery.
  • Recommended dose reductions may include 250 mg twice daily and then 200 mg twice daily.
  • Reduce the dose in patients with moderate renal impairment.
  • Discontinue treatment if MDS/AML is confirmed.

Monitoring

  • Complete Blood Count (CBC).
  • Hemoglobin and other hematologic parameters.
  • Renal function.
  • Liver Function Tests (LFTs).
  • Signs and symptoms of MDS/AML.
  • Signs and symptoms of pneumonitis.
  • Tumor response and disease progression.
