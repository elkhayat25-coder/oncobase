EGFR Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitor

Gilotrif (afatinib)

Boehringer Ingelheim

Gilotrif is a second-generation, irreversible ErbB family blocker indicated for the treatment of metastatic EGFR mutation-positive non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC).

Brand

Gilotrif

Generic

Afatinib

Company

Boehringer Ingelheim

Route

Oral

Approval

FDA Approved

Indications

  • First-line treatment of metastatic NSCLC with EGFR exon 19 deletion or exon 21 (L858R) substitution mutations.
  • Metastatic squamous NSCLC progressing after platinum-based chemotherapy.

Mechanism of Action

Afatinib is an irreversible tyrosine kinase inhibitor that blocks signaling from the ErbB receptor family (EGFR/HER1, HER2 and HER4), suppressing tumor cell proliferation and survival.

Dosage & Administration

Recommended Dose 40 mg once daily
Route Oral
Food Take at least 1 hour before or 2 hours after a meal.
Dose Reduction Reduce by 10 mg increments if needed.

Dose Modifications

  • Interrupt therapy for Grade ≥3 adverse reactions.
  • Resume at a reduced dose after recovery.
  • Permanently discontinue for severe ILD or life-threatening toxicity.

Monitoring

  • Diarrhea and dehydration.
  • Skin toxicity.
  • Liver function tests.
  • Signs of interstitial lung disease.

Drug Interactions

  • P-glycoprotein (P-gp) inhibitors.
  • P-glycoprotein (P-gp) inducers.
  • Avoid concomitant administration when possible.
