CDK4/6 Inhibitor

Kisqali (ribociclib)

Novartis

Kisqali (ribociclib) is an oral CDK4/6 inhibitor indicated for the treatment of HR-positive, HER2-negative advanced or metastatic breast cancer in combination with endocrine therapy.

Brand

Kisqali

Generic

Ribociclib

Company

Novartis

Route

Oral

Approval

FDA Approved

Indications

  • HR-positive, HER2-negative advanced breast cancer.
  • Metastatic breast cancer in combination with aromatase inhibitors.
  • Combination with fulvestrant after disease progression.

Mechanism of Action

Ribociclib selectively inhibits cyclin-dependent kinases 4 and 6 (CDK4/6), preventing phosphorylation of the retinoblastoma protein (Rb) and arresting tumor cells in the G1 phase of the cell cycle.

Dosing

Recommended dose: 600 mg orally once daily for 21 days followed by 7 days off treatment.

Dose Modifications

  • Interrupt treatment for Grade 3 or 4 neutropenia.
  • Reduce dose if toxicity recurs.
  • Monitor ECG and liver function tests regularly.

Monitoring

  • Complete Blood Count (CBC).
  • Liver Function Tests (LFTs).
