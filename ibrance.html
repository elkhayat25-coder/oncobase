CDK4/6 Inhibitor

Ibrance (palbociclib)

Pfizer

Ibrance (palbociclib) is an oral CDK4/6 inhibitor indicated for HR-positive, HER2-negative advanced or metastatic breast cancer in combination with endocrine therapy.

Brand

Ibrance

Generic

Palbociclib

Class

CDK4/6 Inhibitor

Route

Oral

Company

Pfizer

Indications

  • HR-positive, HER2-negative advanced breast cancer.
  • Metastatic breast cancer in combination with aromatase inhibitors.
  • Combination with fulvestrant after disease progression.

Mechanism of Action

Palbociclib inhibits cyclin-dependent kinases 4 and 6 (CDK4/6), preventing phosphorylation of the retinoblastoma (Rb) protein. This blocks cell cycle progression from G1 to S phase and inhibits tumor cell proliferation.

Dosing

Recommended dose: 125 mg orally once daily for 21 days followed by 7 days off treatment.

Route Oral

Combination Endocrine Therapy

Dose Modifications

  • 125 mg → 100 mg → 75 mg.
  • Interrupt treatment for Grade 3/4 neutropenia.
  • Resume at reduced dose if toxicity recurs.

Monitoring

  • Complete Blood Count (CBC).
  • Absolute Neutrophil Count (ANC).
  • Signs of infection.
  • Treatment-related toxicities.
